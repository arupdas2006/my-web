Where the flow breaks

  1. 1

    Checks at the VHND are too weak to find HRPs

    Hb is read off a colour-strip scale, BP devices are not calibrated, and there is no urine test. The VHND is still run mainly for immunisation.

    EvidenceHb in the ANM's records bunches on whole numbers (10, 11, 12). The CHC HRP register has lab values such as 9.2, 9.6, 10.1 and 6.7. Urine is recorded as "Nil" on the MCP card and in the immunisation register, although there is no kit at the VHND.
    Text 1–2

  2. 2

    Four records, one digitised

    The ANM fills the MCP card, her own ANC notebook, the immunisation register and the RCH register, plus a child due list. Only the RCH register goes into e-Kavach, for ANC data. From the ASHA’s VHIR, only the household enumeration is used in e-Kavach.

    EvidenceAll four carry the same core fields (see the matrix below). The text names three tools; the photos show four, plus the due list.
    Text 3

  3. 3

    The copies drift apart

    Writing the same values again and again creates conflicts, even in one ANM's own books.

    EvidenceOne woman has LMP 8.3.26 in the immunisation register and 3.3.26 in the ANC notebook, a 5-day difference. She is matched on mobile number, ASHA and village, with weight 44 kg and Hb 12 in both. The handwriting is small, so confidence is moderate.

  4. 4

    A risk sign is recorded but not flagged

    The MCP card is the only ANM record with a high-risk yes/no box, and in this card the box is not marked.

    EvidenceWeight 35 kg at height 155 cm (BMI about 14.6) at the first visit on 16.9.26. Only one ANC visit is filled. The ANC grid has 5 columns, not 4 as the text says; the 5th header is not legible.
    Text 3check: 4 or 5 slots

  5. 5

    CHC doctors start from zero

    OPD and ANC doctors do not rely on the ANM's values and repeat Hb, urine albumin and blood sugar. Ultrasound is done at some CHCs or privately.

    EvidenceThe prescription slip has stamps for Hb, blood group, VDRL, HIV/HBsAg and urine sugar/albumin. CBC and USG are written in, and the slip is stamped "HRP".
    Text 4

  6. 6

    CHC data stop at the register

    The CHC ANC and HRP registers are not entered into e-Kavach. Only counts go to HMIS, so HMIS and e-Kavach never match.

    EvidenceThe CHC ANC register seems to have complication columns (anaemia, high BP, bleeding), all blank in the photo. The HRP register records risk codes 1–11 for each woman, but none of this reaches e-Kavach.
    Text 5check: risk columns

  7. 7

    No route back to the field

    Reviews compare HMIS with e-Kavach and track ABHA and PMJAY. No list of identified HRPs goes back to the ANM or ASHA, so follow-up does not happen.

    EvidenceFrom field observation; the photos cannot show this. Review minutes or an HMIS vs e-Kavach extract would document it.
    Text 6

Proposed ANC mechanism with MMSSapp

How a pregnant woman moves from registration to postnatal care, and where the WhatsApp-based MMSSapp supports each step.

Community (sub-center / village, rural and urban) Facility (CHC, PHC or higher) Digital (MMSSapp platform)
1

Early registration Community

Self-registration by the woman, or assisted registration by the ASHA, ANM, AWW or Gram Panchayat members, on MMSSapp.

  • Pregnancy confirmation, name, mobile number
  • Aadhaar and bank details
  • Age, pregnancy history, last menstrual period (LMP)

One-time information, essential for planning.

The ANM does not conduct any ANC check-up.

ASHA / ANM mobilizes the woman · 102/108 pick-up and drop
2

First ANC by the medical officer Facility

Done under the supervision of a medical officer at a CHC, PHC or higher facility.

  • Medical officer prepares the pregnancy care plan with follow-up visit dates
  • Findings recorded on the ANC prescription / Clinic Encounter Form (Annex-1)

Prescription data goes digital Digital

Data from the prescription is transferred directly to the platform by optical character recognition (OCR) or a data entry interface.

↻ Repeats for every follow-up visit
4

Schedule and alerts Digital

A visit schedule is generated from the first ANC data. Alerts go to:

  • the woman, the ASHA and the ANM
  • EMRI (102/108) for transport
  • the facility
Woman reaches the facility on the follow-up day · 102/108
5

Subsequent ANC Facility

Services given according to the stage of pregnancy, using standard pregnancy management guidelines. Recorded on the encounter form.

Longitudinal tracking of mother and child Digital

The same record follows the woman through all phases, and is used for monitoring:

  • Pregnancy
  • Delivery
  • Postnatal care

  • High-risk color code
  • Alert 30 days before the expected date of delivery
  • Closed-loop referral
  • Birth plan

Sources and limits

Twelve photos taken on 7 Oct 2026 at sub-centre Devti, village Gauriya Khurd, and CHC Mohanlalganj, Lucknow, read alongside six written field findings. The photos show women's names, phone numbers and IDs, so share this page only within the team.

Values were read from handwriting, so single digits may be wrong. Three readings need checking against the originals: the number of ANC slots on the MCP card, the blank risk columns in the CHC ANC register, and the "H.R." column in the ANM's notebook, which reads as pulse (77–121) and not as a high-risk mark.

"Times written" counts the paper records in this sample that hold the field. It does not include e-Kavach or HMIS entry.
